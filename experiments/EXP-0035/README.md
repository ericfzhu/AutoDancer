# EXP-0035: Bounded learning-bottleneck diagnosis

The protocol is in `experiment.yaml`. Preparation must establish comparable
Death Metal starts and intermediate frozen-source competence before committing
the six-run training budget. Runtime evidence belongs in
`runs/learning-bottleneck-exp0035`; the final decision will distinguish a completed
learning comparison from a preparation-gated frozen-policy diagnosis.

The preparation gate failed: stochastic direct starts completed 0/54 episodes,
versus 6/6 known trace handoffs. The secondary deterministic check scored 0/27
and 3/3 respectively. No training runs were launched; the final-test pool was
not evaluated by a policy. See `docs/learning-bottleneck-experiment.md` for the
measured scope, action diagnostics, costs, and limitations.
