# EXP-0020: Legal Death Metal guide acquisition

Earlier assisted Zone 2 results were invalid because the environment directly
changed boss-objective health. This experiment makes a hard semantic cut: only
Bard's starting health may change, and the bridge must prove that every boss and
boss-add objective is byte-for-byte equivalent before and after assistance.

The experiment is an acquisition test, not a claim that A8 is generally better
than A2. A8 starts with exactly the selected A2 actor/recurrent function and a
fresh critic. It is used because its zero-projected adapter can begin learning
the richer schema-10 boss and map signals without perturbing the inherited policy
on transition zero.

Passing means that a legally generated guide can repeatedly reach deep Death
Metal phases. The next experiments must shrink the guide horizon from phase 4 to
phase 3, then phase 2, then the full boss start, and finally expand backward to
normal Zone 1 starts. Only repeated Zone 2 entry from unseen normal starts can
satisfy the project goal.
