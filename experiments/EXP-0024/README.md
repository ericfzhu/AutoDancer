# EXP-0024: full-boss terminal-canceling Death Metal acquisition

EXP-0023's legal guide handoff was valid but spent 98.3% of sampled live states
on excluded guide actions. EXP-0024 keeps the exact source policy, player20-only
assistance, untouched boss, V5 state potential, and held-out gameplay criteria,
but begins learner collection at the full boss start. Every action is therefore
an on-policy PPO transition.

The experiment asks whether removing renewable damage return is enough for an
already competent local policy to convert contact into repeatable full clears.
Even a pass is only a downstream acquisition result; assistance contraction,
other Zone 1 bosses, earlier floors, and unseen normal starts remain mandatory.
