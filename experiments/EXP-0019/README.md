# EXP-0019: Stable player8 bridge stage

EXP-0018 showed that direct player10-to-player6 replay can produce an excellent
checkpoint but not reproducibly: its three held-out sampled completion rates were
43.75%, 64.58%, and 33.33%. This experiment inserts player8 as the predeclared
intermediate health stage and expands the finite training pool from nine to 24
telemetry-confirmed Death Metal seeds.

Passing proves only a stable normal-adjacent one-hit boss subskill. It authorizes
a separate player8-to-player6 experiment; it does not authorize boss-health
removal or normal-start promotion.
